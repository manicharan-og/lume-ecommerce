Our approach

LUMÉ is a contemporary clothing concept built around a simple idea: clothing should support personal expression rather than overwhelm it. Our visual language is minimal, calm and everyday.

The collection

The current collection focuses on versatile shirts, T-shirts, trousers and hoodies, with an emphasis on clean silhouettes and easy styling.

The digital experience

The LUMÉ website includes product discovery, saved favourites, shopping bag tools, account features, order tracking and an AI Stylist concept designed to make browsing easier.

Current project status

Important: The current website is a project/demo environment. Payment interfaces are demonstrative and are not a substitute for a production payment gateway. Commercial policies and fulfilment details should be finalised before accepting real customer purchases.
