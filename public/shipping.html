Shipping.

Information about the current LUMÉ delivery experience and what must be finalised for commercial launch.

LAST UPDATED · 8 SEPTEMBER 2026

ON THIS PAGE

Delivery method

The current checkout experience displays free standard delivery. Final carrier, service area and fulfilment timelines have not yet been configured for commercial launch.

Delivery timing

Delivery estimates should be communicated once the order is confirmed and the production fulfilment process is connected. Avoid relying on a specific delivery date unless it is explicitly confirmed.

Delivery address

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address and phone number. Saved addresses can be managed from the LUMÉ Profile page.

Order tracking

The My Orders page displays application-level order status such as Placed, Confirmed, Shipped, Out for Delivery and Delivered when those statuses are updated.

Before commercial launch

Before accepting real orders, LUMÉ should add a real logistics workflow, delivery service areas, estimated delivery windows, support contact information and rules for failed or delayed deliveries.
